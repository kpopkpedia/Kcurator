About K-pedia

More than K-pop.
The Story of Korean Popular Music.

Welcome to K-pedia, an English-language archive dedicated to the history, artists, industry, concerts, and culture of Korean popular music.

Korean popular music is far more diverse than many people realize. While K-pop has become a global phenomenon through idol groups, its story also includes legendary ballad singers, rock musicians, hip-hop artists, indie bands, soundtrack composers, producers, and countless performers who have shaped Korean music across generations.

At K-pedia, we believe that every part of that story deserves to be documented.

Our goal is to provide well-researched, objective, and accessible articles that help readers understand not only today's global K-pop industry but also the broader musical landscape that made it possible. We explore the evolution of Korean popular music—from the rise of modern pop in the late 20th century to today's worldwide concert tours, festivals, and digital music culture.

What K-pedia Covers

  • K-pop artists and idol groups
  • Legendary singers and influential musicians
  • Rock, ballad, hip-hop, R&B, indie, and band music
  • Original Soundtracks (OSTs)
  • World tours, concerts, and live events
  • Entertainment companies and the Korean music industry
  • The trainee system, music production, and fan culture
  • Music history, cultural milestones, and notable events

Rather than focusing on celebrity gossip or rumors, K-pedia emphasizes context, history, and reliable information. We strive to explain how Korean music has evolved, how the industry operates, and why certain artists, songs, albums, and performances have become significant in Korea and around the world.

Whether you're discovering Korean music for the first time, researching the history of K-pop, following your favorite artist's latest tour, or exploring classic musicians who influenced today's generation, K-pedia aims to be a trusted resource that goes beyond the headlines.

Korean popular music continues to evolve, and so will this archive. As new artists emerge and new chapters are written, K-pedia will continue documenting the people, music, performances, and stories that define one of the world's most dynamic music industries.

Thank you for visiting K-pedia. We hope you'll enjoy exploring the past, present, and future of Korean popular music with us.
