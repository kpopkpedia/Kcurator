About K-pedia

More than K-pop.
The Story of Korean Popular Music.

Welcome to K-pedia, an English-language archive dedicated to the history, artists, industry, concerts, and culture of Korean popular music.

Korean popular music is far more diverse than many people realize. While K-pop has become a global phenomenon through idol groups, its story also includes legendary ballad singers, rock musicians, hip-hop artists, indie bands, soundtrack composers, producers, and countless performers who have shaped Korean music across generations.

K-pedia was created to document that broader story—and, more importantly, to provide the cultural and historical context that is often missing from English-language coverage of Korean music.

More Than a K-pop Encyclopedia

A song is rarely important simply because it was popular. Behind it are changes in television, technology, youth culture, fashion, the music industry, and the ways people discovered and listened to music.

Rather than simply collecting artist profiles, discographies, or chart information, K-pedia explores why particular artists, songs, albums, performances, and cultural moments mattered—and how they fit into the broader history of Korean popular culture.

A Korean Perspective

K-pedia is written and curated by a Korean writer and editor who experienced many of these cultural changes firsthand and now lives in Canada.

Growing up with Korean popular music means remembering not only the songs themselves, but also how people encountered them: through television music programs, radio, cassette tapes, record stores, films, dramas, concerts, and the conversations surrounding them.

K-pedia combines that lived cultural memory with research and historical sources to make the history of Korean popular music accessible to international readers. Looking at Korean music from both inside and outside Korea also provides a perspective on how a largely domestic popular culture developed into a global one.

What K-pedia Covers

  • K-pop artists and idol groups
  • Legendary singers and influential musicians
  • Rock, ballad, hip-hop, R&B, indie, and band music
  • Film and television soundtracks
  • World tours, concerts, and live events
  • Entertainment companies and the Korean music industry
  • Broadcasting, fandom, and music culture
  • Historical events and cultural moments that shaped Korean music

Editorial Approach

K-pedia emphasizes context, history, and reliable information rather than celebrity gossip or rumors. Articles are researched using available records, contemporary reporting, broadcasts, official materials, and other relevant sources.

When historical accounts differ or information cannot be independently confirmed, K-pedia aims to avoid presenting uncertain claims as established fact. The goal is not simply to document what happened, but to preserve the context that helps explain why it mattered.

Whether you're discovering Korean music for the first time, researching the history of K-pop, or exploring the artists and cultural moments that influenced today's generation, K-pedia aims to provide a deeper way to understand Korean popular music.

Korean popular music continues to evolve, and so will this archive.
