The soundtrack to **Love For Love (사랑을 위하여)**, the official soundtrack album for the KBS weekend drama of the same name, offers a nostalgic look at the sound of Korean television in the early 1990s.

Released in March 1993, the album brings together actors, folk musicians, and pop vocalists in a collection of ballads and instrumental pieces that reflect the emotional storytelling style of the era.

# About the Drama

*Love For Love (사랑을 위하여)* aired on KBS from 1992 to 1993 during a period when family dramas were among the most popular genres on Korean television.

Like many television productions of the time, the series released an original soundtrack album featuring vocal songs alongside instrumental themes used throughout the drama.

---

# The Soundtrack & Featured Artists

Rather than focusing on a single singer, the album features several performers with different musical backgrounds:

- **Na Hyun-hee (나현희):** Best known as an actress, she also pursued a brief singing career during the early 1990s.
- **Shin Gye-haeng (신계행):** Respected folk singer-songwriter.
- **Oh Cheol-jin (오철진):** Ballad vocalist.
- **Kim Kyung-hee (김경희):** Pop vocalist.
- **Saekjongi (색종이):** Vocal group.

Together, their performances create a soundtrack built around gentle ballads, folk influences, and understated instrumental themes that complement the drama's emotional tone.

# Featured Song: "I Won't Love You"

🎥 Na Hyun-hee – "I Won't Love You" (사랑하지 않을거야) | Stage Mix Compilation (1993)

A classic 1993 TV stage performance by actress and singer Na Hyun-hee.

Among the album's best-remembered songs is **"I Won't Love You" (사랑하지 않을거야)**, performed by actress Na Hyun-hee.

The song emphasizes emotional restraint rather than dramatic vocal techniques, making it representative of the sentimental ballad style frequently heard in Korean television dramas during the early 1990s.

The melody also appears in both violin and piano arrangements, allowing the soundtrack to revisit its central musical theme throughout the drama.

# Why It Matters

*Love For Love* may not be among Korea's most famous drama soundtracks today, but it represents an important period in the history of Korean television music.

Its combination of an actress, folk musicians, pop vocalists, and instrumental themes reflects how drama soundtracks were produced before the Korean Wave transformed Korean entertainment into a global phenomenon.

---

### Curator's Note

For modern listeners, *Love For Love* offers more than nostalgia. It provides a glimpse into a time when Korean drama soundtracks focused on supporting the story with understated melodies rather than producing chart-topping hit singles.
