K-pop idol positions generally fall into **two broad categories**: **Music & Performance Positions**, which define a member's artistic role on stage, and **Group Identity & Team Roles**, which shape the group's public image, internal dynamics, and overall identity.

Unlike many Western pop groups, K-pop teams often assign specialized roles to individual members, helping audiences understand each member's strengths while creating a balanced group dynamic.

---

# Music & Performance Positions

Performance positions primarily describe a member's artistic responsibilities in singing, dancing, and rapping. Most groups designate **Main** and **Lead** positions, while other members contribute in supporting roles depending on each song.

## Vocal Positions

### Main Vocal

**The strongest vocalist in the group.**

The Main Vocal is responsible for the most technically demanding parts of a song, including climactic high notes, emotional peaks, and challenging live performances.

### Lead Vocal

**Usually the second strongest vocalist.**

Lead Vocals perform substantial melodic sections throughout a song and often share important lines with the Main Vocal while helping carry the overall melody.

### Supporting Vocal (Sub Vocal)

Supporting vocalists contribute throughout the song with verses, bridges, harmonies, background vocals, and additional melodic lines.

Although they may receive fewer technically demanding parts, supporting vocalists remain essential to the group's overall sound.

## Dance Positions

### Main Dancer

![](/assets/images/2026_08/lisa.jpeg)

LISA – Main Dancer of BLACKPINK

**The group's strongest dancer.**

Main Dancers often lead dance breaks, solo performances, and technically demanding choreography while helping establish the overall performance quality.

### Lead Dancer

Lead Dancers perform alongside the Main Dancer and frequently occupy prominent positions within formations, helping maintain synchronization and visual balance.

**🪧 Note:** *Unlike vocal and rap positions, "Sub Dancer" is rarely used as an official designation in K-pop.*

---

## Rap Positions

### Main Rapper

![](/assets/images/2026_08/jennie.jpg)

JENNIE – Main Rapper of BLACKPINK

The group's primary rapper, typically possessing the strongest flow, rhythm, diction, and stage delivery.

Main Rappers usually perform the longest and most technically demanding rap sections.

### Lead Rapper

Lead Rappers support the Main Rapper by performing additional rap verses and may also contribute as vocalists depending on the group's musical style.

### Supporting Rapper (Sub Rapper)

Supporting rappers perform shorter rap sections or rhythmic spoken parts that complement the overall arrangement.

# Group Identity & Team Roles

Beyond musical performance, K-pop groups often develop distinctive internal roles that strengthen teamwork, public identity, and fan engagement.

Some of these positions are officially assigned, while others emerge naturally through group dynamics.

## Leader

![](/assets/images/2026_08/gd.jpg)

G-DRAGON – Leader & Main Rapper of BIGBANG

The Leader serves as the team's representative and primary spokesperson.

**Responsibilities often include:**

- Speaking during interviews and award ceremonies
- Coordinating communication among members
- Acting as the primary liaison with the agency
- Helping maintain team unity and morale

Although leadership styles differ, the Leader is often viewed as the group's anchor both on and off stage.

## Eldest Member (Hyung / Unnie)

![](/assets/images/2026_08/jinbts.jpg)

JIN – Eldest Member of BTS

Although **not an official position**, the eldest member often plays an important cultural role within the group.

In Korean culture, age carries social significance, and the eldest member frequently serves as a stabilizing presence by:

- Offering emotional support
- Sharing experience
- Helping resolve conflicts
- Creating a comfortable atmosphere for younger members

In many groups, the eldest member complements the Leader by providing quiet support behind the scenes.

## Center

![](/assets/images/2026_08/yoona.webp)

YOONA – Center of Girls' Generation

The Center serves as the visual and performance focal point during key moments of a performance.

**Centers frequently appear in:**

- Opening formations
- Chorus highlights
- Ending poses
- Promotional materials

Some groups have a **fixed center**, while others rotate the center position depending on the choreography, song, or concept.

## Visual / Face of the Group

![](/assets/images/2026_08/chaeunwoo.jpeg)

Cha Eun-woo – Visual of ASTRO

The Visual represents the group's public image.

**Visual members are often featured prominently in:**

- Advertisements
- Fashion campaigns
- Magazine editorials
- Acting projects
- Promotional content

While physical appearance is an important factor, charisma, screen presence, and public recognition also contribute to this role.

## Maknae

![](/assets/images/2026_08/jungkook.jpg)

JUNGKOOK – Maknae (Golden Maknae) of BTS

The Maknae is the youngest member of the group.

Although traditionally associated with youthful charm, today's maknaes display a wide range of personalities—from playful and affectionate to confident and charismatic.

Fans often celebrate unique dynamics such as the **"maknae on top"**, referring to younger members who confidently tease or lead their older teammates.
