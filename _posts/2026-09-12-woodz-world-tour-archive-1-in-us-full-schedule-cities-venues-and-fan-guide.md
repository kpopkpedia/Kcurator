# About WOODZ

**WOODZ (Cho Seung-youn)** is a South Korean singer-songwriter, producer, and performer recognized for his versatility across alternative rock, R&B, pop, hip-hop, and electronic music.

After making his solo debut in 2016, he established himself as one of Korea's most respected self-producing artists through his songwriting, composing, and live performances. Before focusing on his solo career, he was also active as a member of UNIQ and the project group X1.

In 2024, WOODZ joined **EDAM Entertainment**, beginning a new chapter in his career while continuing to build an international fanbase through acclaimed albums and sold-out concerts.
