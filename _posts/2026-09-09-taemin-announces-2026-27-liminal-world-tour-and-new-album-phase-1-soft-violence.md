South Korean singer and SHINee member **TAEMIN** has officially announced his return with a new album and the first phase of his **2026–27 TAEMIN WORLD TOUR LiMiNaL**.

The comeback begins with the release of his new album, *PHASE 1 : SOFT VIOLENCE*, on August 31, 2026, followed by a world tour that will launch in Seoul before expanding across North and South America. The initial announcement includes ten overseas cities, with additional destinations expected to be revealed in future phases.

---

# Post Highlights

### New Album: PHASE 1 : SOFT VIOLENCE

TAEMIN's latest solo album will be released on August 31, 2026, at 6:00 PM (KST). The project marks the beginning of a new musical era that will also serve as the foundation for the upcoming LiMiNaL world tour.

### 2026–27 LiMiNaL World Tour

The tour will begin in Seoul before continuing to North and South America, making it one of TAEMIN's largest international tours to date.

### Phase 1 Announcement

The first tour announcement includes six cities in the United States and four cities across Latin America. Additional tour legs are expected to be announced separately.

### New Performances

Concert audiences will be among the first to experience live performances from *PHASE 1 : SOFT VIOLENCE*, alongside TAEMIN's established solo repertoire.

# About the Comeback

*PHASE 1 : SOFT VIOLENCE* marks TAEMIN's latest solo release and the beginning of a new touring cycle.

Since making his solo debut, TAEMIN has built a reputation for combining contemporary dance, theatrical stage production, and experimental pop music. His solo work has consistently emphasized visual storytelling and performance-driven concepts, establishing him as one of K-pop's most distinctive solo artists.

The LiMiNaL world tour is expected to continue that artistic direction while introducing music from the new album to audiences around the world.

---

# Phase 1 Tour Cities

The first phase of the tour begins in Seoul before expanding to ten cities across North and South America:

- **South Korea:** Seoul
- **United States:** San Jose, Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Grand Prairie, Chicago, New York
- **Mexico:** Monterrey, Mexico City
- **Chile:** Santiago
- **Peru:** Lima

At the time of the initial announcement, detailed concert dates, venues, and ticketing schedules had not yet been released.

---

# What We Know So Far

The current announcement represents Phase 1 of the LiMiNaL world tour.

Rather than unveiling a complete global itinerary at once, the initial announcement focuses on Seoul and the Americas. This phased rollout has become increasingly common for large-scale international K-pop tours, allowing organizers to reveal additional regions over time.

Although no further stops have been confirmed, future announcements are widely expected to include cities in Asia, Europe, and Oceania.

---

# Why It Matters

The LiMiNaL tour marks TAEMIN's next major chapter as a solo performer following the release of *PHASE 1 : SOFT VIOLENCE*.

Beyond promoting a new album, the tour reflects TAEMIN's continued presence as one of K-pop's leading performance artists. His concerts have become known for blending choreography, cinematic visuals, and live performance into a unified artistic experience, making each tour a significant milestone in his solo career.
