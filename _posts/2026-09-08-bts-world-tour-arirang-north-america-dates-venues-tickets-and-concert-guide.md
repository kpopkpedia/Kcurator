Following the completion of all seven members' military service, BTS returns with **BTS WORLD TOUR 'ARIRANG'**, a large-scale stadium tour spanning Asia, North America, Europe, and beyond.

The North American leg consists of **two separate stadium runs**: a spring leg in April–May and a late-summer leg in August–September.

# ARMY Membership Presale

Fans with an active **Weverse ARMY Membership** may apply for the official presale before general ticket sales begin.

The process generally includes:

1. Join Weverse ARMY Membership.
2. Apply during the designated presale registration period.
3. Receive a presale code if selected.
4. Purchase tickets during the presale window.

Any remaining tickets become available during the **General Onsale**.

# Concert Day Tips

To make your concert experience smoother:

- Arrive at least **2–3 hours before** showtime.
- Download your mobile ticket before arriving.
- Review your venue's bag policy and prohibited items.
- Bring a portable phone charger.
- Wear comfortable shoes, as stadium venues often require extensive walking.
- Consider public transportation or pre-book parking when available.

---

# Official Merchandise

Official BTS merchandise is typically available:

- At outdoor merchandise booths before gates open
- Inside the stadium after entry

Limited-edition items and popular products often sell out quickly, so arriving early is recommended.

# Before You Go

Before traveling to the venue, check:

- Your ticket platform account for any updates
- Stadium entry and security policies
- Parking and public transportation information
- Weather forecasts for your concert date

Schedules, venue policies, and ticketing procedures are subject to change. Fans should always refer to official BTS, Weverse, and venue announcements for the latest information.
