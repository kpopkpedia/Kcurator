South Korean indie band **wave to earth** is returning to the global stage with **"the pieces tour,"** their biggest international headline tour to date. Following the announcement of a new album and the release of the single **"heaven and hell,"** the trio will perform across North America and Asia, including landmark venues such as **Radio City Music Hall** in New York and **Singapore Indoor Stadium**.

Known for their dreamy blend of indie rock, jazz, lo-fi, and alternative pop influences, wave to earth has built a dedicated international following through its atmospheric sound and emotionally resonant performances. The 2026 tour represents another major milestone as the band continues to expand its global audience.

---

## About wave to earth

Formed in Seoul, **wave to earth** consists of vocalist and guitarist **Daniel Kim**, bassist **John Cha**, and drummer **Shin Donggyu**. The trio has earned worldwide recognition for its warm analog sound, intimate songwriting, and distinctive live performances.

Unlike many Korean acts that focus primarily on domestic promotions, wave to earth has steadily built an international fanbase through overseas tours, music festivals, and streaming platforms. Their music has attracted listeners well beyond Korea, making them one of the country's most recognized contemporary indie bands.
