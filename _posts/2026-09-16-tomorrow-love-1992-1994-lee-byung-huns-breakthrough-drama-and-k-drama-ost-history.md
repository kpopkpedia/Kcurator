Premiering on KBS 2TV in November 1992, ***Tomorrow Love* (내일은 사랑)** became one of the defining campus dramas of the early 1990s. Airing for 103 episodes over nearly two years, the series captured the friendships, romances, ambitions, and everyday lives of Korean university students during a period of rapid social change.



The drama is also remembered as one of the earliest breakthrough works of **Lee Byung-hun**, who would later become one of South Korea's most internationally acclaimed actors. Its soundtrack—particularly Lee's performance of **"Smile of a Rose" (장미의 미소)**—helped demonstrate how music could become an es    sential part of a youth drama's identity.

# Post Highlights

**Campus Drama Classic:** One of the defining Korean university dramas of the 1990s, portraying friendship, romance, and the hopes of young adulthood.

**Future Global Star:** Helped establish **Lee Byung-hun**, now one of South Korea's most internationally recognized actors, while introducing audiences to a generation of rising stars.

**Actor-Performed Soundtrack:** Featured Lee Byung-hun performing **"Smile of a Rose" (장미의 미소)**, one of the most memorable actor-performed drama songs of the early 1990s.

**The Sound of Campus Life:** Popularized bright acoustic pop and sentimental ballads that perfectly matched the atmosphere of Korean university life.

# Drama Synopsis

Set on a lively university campus, *Tomorrow Love* follows a close-knit group of students as they navigate friendship, first love, academic life, and the uncertainty of adulthood. Rather than focusing on extraordinary events, the drama resonated with viewers by portraying the everyday experiences and aspirations of Korean college students in the early 1990s.

---

# The Central Characters

## Shin Bum-soo (Lee Byung-hun)

An outgoing, witty, and charismatic architecture student, Bum-soo serves as the emotional center of the series. The role became one of Lee Byung-hun's earliest breakthrough performances, helping launch the career of the actor who would later achieve worldwide recognition through acclaimed films such as *Joint Security Area*, *A Bittersweet Life*, and *I Saw the Devil*, as well as international productions including *G.I. Joe*, *The Magnificent Seven*, and Netflix's *Squid Game*.

---

## Yoo Hyun-kyung (Ko So-young)

Appearing in the early episodes, Hyun-kyung is portrayed as an independent and confident university student whose on-screen chemistry with Bum-soo quickly became popular among viewers before Ko So-young departed from the series.

---

## Han Hye-bin (Park So-hyun)

Following Hyun-kyung's departure, Hye-bin enters the story as a thoughtful and compassionate student who gradually becomes Bum-soo's primary love interest, forming one of the drama's most beloved couples.

---

## The Campus Ensemble

Supporting actors including **Kim Jung-kyun, Lee Ji-hyung, and Lee Kyung-shim** portrayed a diverse group of classmates whose friendships, student clubs, and campus activities reflected the everyday life of Korean university students during the early 1990s. Their natural performances contributed significantly to the drama's lasting appeal and realism.

---

# Director Yoon Seok-ho's Early Career

Co-directed by **Yoon Seok-ho** and **Lee Young-hee**, *Tomorrow Love* occupies an important place in the early careers of both directors. For Yoon Seok-ho in particular, the series showcased the emotional storytelling, gentle visual style, and close integration of music and romance that would later define internationally celebrated Hallyu classics such as *Autumn in My Heart* (2000) and *Winter Sonata* (2002).

## "Smile of a Rose" — An Actor's Signature Song

The soundtrack's most enduring song is **"Smile of a Rose" (장미의 미소)**, performed by Lee Byung-hun. Written by Shin Dong-woo, the gentle acoustic pop ballad perfectly captured the innocence and optimism associated with university life in early-1990s Korea. The song became one of the most beloved drama-related songs of its era and remains closely associated with both the series and Lee Byung-hun's early career.

---

## Capturing the Sound of 1990s Youth

Unlike the grand orchestral style of historical dramas such as *Eyes of Dawn* (1991), *Tomorrow Love* embraced acoustic guitars, light pop arrangements, and sentimental ballads. Songs including **"Tomorrow Love"**, composed and performed by Shin Dong-woo, reflected the youthful energy and romantic atmosphere that resonated with audiences throughout the 1990s.

---

## Popularizing Actor-Performed Drama Songs

The popularity of Lee Byung-hun's performance demonstrated that actors could successfully contribute to a drama's musical identity. Throughout the 1990s, it became increasingly common for lead actors to record songs for their own dramas, further strengthening the connection between television storytelling and popular music.

---

# Legacy

More than three decades after its original broadcast, *Tomorrow Love* remains a nostalgic portrait of Korean campus life in the early 1990s—a world of student festivals, acoustic guitars, first love, and youthful ambition.



Building upon the popularity of earlier campus dramas such as ***Our Paradise* (1990)**, ***Tomorrow Love*** helped further establish the campus drama as one of Korean television's defining genres during the 1990s. Alongside its memorable soundtrack, the series demonstrated how music could deepen the emotional appeal of youth-oriented storytelling and contributed to the continuing evolution of Korean drama OSTs.
