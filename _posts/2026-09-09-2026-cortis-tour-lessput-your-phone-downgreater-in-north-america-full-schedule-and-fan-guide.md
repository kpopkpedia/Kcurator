Rookie boy group **CORTIS** is making its highly anticipated North American touring debut with the **2026 CORTIS TOUR <PUT YOUR PHONE DOWN> IN NORTH AMERICA**, bringing the group's youthful energy, performance-focused style, and creative identity to six major cities across the United States and Canada.

## About the PUT YOUR PHONE DOWN Tour

**PUT YOUR PHONE DOWN** is CORTIS's first official concert tour, marking an important step in the group's transition from a newly debuted act to an international touring artist.

After opening in **Incheon, South Korea**, the tour continues across North America before expanding to additional international markets. The production is expected to highlight the group's creative identity through choreography, visual concepts, member interaction, and performances drawn from its early discography.

The tour title reflects CORTIS's youthful and direct approach to connecting with its audience, inviting fans to step away from their screens and experience the music together in a live setting.

## About CORTIS

**CORTIS** is a five-member South Korean boy group formed under **BIGHIT MUSIC**, the label behind BTS and TOMORROW X TOGETHER.

The group consists of:

- **MARTIN**
- **JAMES**
- **JUHOON**
- **SEONGHYEON**
- **KEONHO**

CORTIS debuted in **August 2025**. The group's name is derived from letters in the phrase **COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES**, representing a determination to think and create beyond conventional boundaries.

Known for combining music, choreography, visual direction, and youthful storytelling, the members have been presented not only as performers but also as active creative participants in the group's work.

## Fan Tips

### Arrive Early for Merchandise

Official merchandise and venue-exclusive items may sell out before the concert begins. Fans interested in purchasing tour goods should arrive early and check whether the venue provides an outdoor merchandise booth or pre-show sales period.

### Prepare Your Mobile Ticket

Ticketmaster advises fans to access their tickets through the Ticketmaster app or add them to a mobile wallet before leaving for the venue. The scannable barcode may not become available until closer to the concert date.

### Check Venue-Specific Entry Rules

Bag restrictions, prohibited items, light stick policies, and security procedures vary by venue. Review the official venue information shortly before the concert rather than assuming that the same rules apply at every stop.

### Allow Extra Time for Security

Large theatres may have substantial entry lines, particularly for sold-out performances. Arriving early allows time for security screening, merchandise purchases, restroom visits, and finding your seat.

### Check Transportation Before the Show

Some venues are located outside the central downtown area. The Los Angeles concert, for example, takes place at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, while the Texas concert is located in Irving rather than central Dallas. Plan parking or public transportation in advance.
