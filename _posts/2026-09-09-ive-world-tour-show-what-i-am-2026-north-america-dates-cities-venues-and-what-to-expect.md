# IVE Brings the 'SHOW WHAT I AM' World Tour to North America

After launching their **SHOW WHAT I AM** World Tour in Asia, K-pop girl group **IVE** is bringing the tour to North America this summer.

Beginning in **Toronto on July 21**, the six-member group will perform across Canada and the United States before concluding the North American leg in **Vancouver on August 9**. The tour includes eight concerts in seven cities, featuring arena performances in major markets such as Toronto, Newark, Los Angeles, Oakland, and Seattle.

# A Bigger North American Tour

Compared with IVE's previous overseas activities, **SHOW WHAT I AM** marks the group's most ambitious North American tour to date.

The itinerary includes performances at several of the continent's premier arenas, including **Scotiabank Arena**, **Kia Forum**, **Oakland Arena**, and **Climate Pledge Arena**. The routing also gives Canadian fans three opportunities to see the group, with concerts in **Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver**.

---

# What Is the 'SHOW WHAT I AM' Tour?

The **SHOW WHAT I AM** World Tour takes its name from IVE's confident message of self-expression and individuality.

Known for hits such as **ELEVEN**, **LOVE DIVE**, **After LIKE**, **I AM**, **Baddie**, **HEYA**, and **REBEL HEART**, IVE has built a reputation for elegant stage production, polished choreography, and strong live performances.

Rather than focusing on a single album, the concert celebrates the group's musical journey since debut while showcasing the confidence that has become central to IVE's identity.

---

# Why This Tour Matters

The North American expansion reflects IVE's growing global popularity.

Since debuting in 2021, the group has become one of the leading fourth-generation K-pop acts, earning multiple Song of the Year awards and building a large international fanbase.

The addition of Canadian cities and major U.S. arenas demonstrates the group's continued growth outside Asia and highlights the increasing demand for K-pop concerts across North America.

---

# Will More Tour Dates Be Added?

As of **July 16, 2026**, eight North American concerts have been officially announced.

No additional U.S. or Canadian dates have been confirmed. Fans should monitor IVE's official channels for future announcements regarding additional concerts or schedule updates.

---

# Final Thoughts

IVE's **SHOW WHAT I AM** World Tour represents another milestone in the group's rapid international expansion.

With stops in eight major arenas across Canada and the United States, the North American leg offers fans an opportunity to experience one of K-pop's fastest-growing girl groups in a full-scale arena production.

Whether you're attending in person or following the tour online, **SHOW WHAT I AM** promises to be one of the standout K-pop tours of 2026.
