# MAMAMOO is officially back together.

The **MAMAMOO 2026 WORLD TOUR [4WARD]** celebrates the group's **12th anniversary** and marks the long-awaited return of **Solar, Moonbyul, Wheein, and Hwasa** as a full four-member group. The tour spans **Asia, North America, and Australia**, with additional cities expected to be announced in the future.

## Tour Highlights

- **12th Anniversary Tour** – Celebrating 12 years since MAMAMOO's debut.
- **Full-Group Reunion** – The members reunite for their first full-group world tour in several years.
- **Inspired by *4WARD*** – The tour shares its name with the group's 2026 special release, symbolizing the four members moving forward together.
- **Global Tour** – The current itinerary includes Asia, North America, and Australia, with more stops expected to be announced.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is this MAMAMOO's first world tour?

A: No. **[4WARD]** is MAMAMOO's **second world tour** and their **first full-group world tour in several years**, following the successful **MY CON** tour.

Q: Will more tour dates be announced?

A: Possibly. Additional concerts have already been added in **Kaohsiung** and **Macau**, and more cities may be announced as the tour continues.

Q: Which cities are included in the North American tour?
