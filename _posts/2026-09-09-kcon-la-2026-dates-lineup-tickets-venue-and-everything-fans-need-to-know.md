KCON, the world's largest K-pop and Korean culture festival, is returning to Los Angeles this summer.

**KCON LA 2026** will take place from **August 14–16** at **[Crypto.com](http://Crypto.com) Arena** and the **Los Angeles Convention Center**, bringing together some of the biggest names in K-pop alongside Korean beauty, fashion, food, gaming, and pop culture experiences.

Unlike a traditional concert, KCON combines live performances, fan meetings, artist interactions, exhibitions, and cultural events into one weekend, making it one of the biggest annual Hallyu festivals outside Korea.

# More Than Just a Concert

One reason KCON has remained successful for more than a decade is that it offers much more than live performances. Visitors can experience:

- Artist Meet & Greets
- Dance workshops
- K-pop cover dance events
- Beauty demonstrations
- Korean food experiences
- Merchandise booths
- Interactive fan activities
- Creator and influencer stages

The 2026 edition also introduces expanded festival grounds and multiple performance stages, including the **M COUNTDOWN Stage**, **Artist Stage**, **X Stage**, and **Dance Stage**.

---

# Why KCON Matters

Since launching in **2012**, KCON has become one of the biggest global events dedicated to Korean entertainment and culture.

What started as a relatively small convention has evolved into a major international festival attracting hundreds of thousands of fans worldwide. Beyond music, KCON has played a significant role in introducing global audiences to Korean fashion, beauty, food, television, and lifestyle, helping expand the reach of the Korean Wave (Hallyu).

For many international fans, attending KCON is often their first opportunity to experience multiple K-pop artists in one event.

# Tickets

Tickets are currently available through the official KCON ticketing platform. Depending on the ticket type, attendees can purchase:

- Convention-only admission
- M COUNTDOWN concert tickets
- Combination packages
- Premium fan experiences (availability varies)

Some artist engagement events may require separate reservations or lotteries. Fans should check the official KCON website for the latest availability and event details.

# Final Thoughts

KCON LA 2026 once again brings together K-pop performances and Korean culture in one of the largest Hallyu festivals outside Korea.

With three days of concerts, fan experiences, cultural programming, and a lineup featuring some of today's biggest K-pop artists, this year's event continues KCON's tradition as one of the highlights of the global K-pop calendar.

Whether you're attending in Los Angeles or following the performances online, KCON LA remains one of the best opportunities each year to experience the diversity of modern Korean pop culture.
