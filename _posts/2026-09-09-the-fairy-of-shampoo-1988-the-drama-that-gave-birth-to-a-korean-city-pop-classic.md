Before Korean drama soundtracks became a global phenomenon, there was ***The Fairy of Shampoo* (샴푸의 요정).**

Broadcast as part of MBC's acclaimed anthology series *Best Theater* on **November 6, 1988**, the single-episode drama introduced viewers to a sophisticated urban romance set in the world of advertising. Its stylish cinematography, contemporary setting, and unforgettable theme song made it one of the most distinctive television productions of the late 1980s.

Today, the drama is remembered not only as a cult classic but also as the origin of one of the most beloved songs in Korean popular music history.

Set inside a newly established advertising agency, *The Fairy of Shampoo* explores the aspirations, relationships, and everyday struggles of young professionals navigating Seoul's rapidly modernizing urban landscape.

Blending reality with fantasy, the story follows characters whose admiration for a glamorous shampoo model reflects the dreams and ideals of a generation experiencing South Korea's changing consumer culture during the late 1980s.

Its refined visual style and understated storytelling gave the drama a uniquely contemporary atmosphere that distinguished it from many television productions of its time.

# The Theme Song That Became a Korean Classic

🎥 Light & Salt – "The Fairy of Shampoo" Stage Performance Collection (KBS Archives)
{% include youtube.html url="[https://www.youtube.com/embed/o21UJxiXOsI](https://www.youtube.com/embed/o21UJxiXOsI)" %}
One of the drama's most enduring legacies is its theme song, "The Fairy of Shampoo."

Written and composed by Jang Ki-ho, the song was created specifically as the theme for the 1988 MBC drama. With its dreamy synthesizers, sophisticated bass lines, and smooth melodies, it perfectly complemented the drama's elegant urban aesthetic.

Following the drama's broadcast, the song was released on Love and Peace's fourth album in 1989. A year later, after Jang Ki-ho and Park Sung-sik formed the jazz-pop duo Light & Salt, they re-recorded the song for the group's debut album (1990). This version became the definitive recording remembered by generations of listeners.

Today, the song is widely regarded as one of the defining works of Korean city pop and remains Light & Salt's signature recording.

Why It Still Matters
Unlike many television themes of its era, The Fairy of Shampoo developed a life far beyond the drama itself.

Its sophisticated blend of jazz, funk, soft rock, and city pop helped shape the sound of Korean popular music during the late 1980s and early 1990s. Decades later, the song continues to inspire musicians and listeners alike, demonstrating how a television soundtrack can transcend its original medium.

A New Life Through K-pop
Interest in The Fairy of Shampoo has continued across generations through numerous reinterpretations.

Among the most internationally recognized is the 2020 remake by TXT (Tomorrow X Together), which reimagined the song with contemporary R&B and dream-pop influences while preserving the nostalgic atmosphere of the original.

# Legacy

Although *The Fairy of Shampoo* was broadcast as a single television drama in 1988, its influence has extended far beyond its original runtime.

The drama introduced a theme song that would evolve into one of Korea's most beloved popular songs, while its sophisticated visual style anticipated the urban sensibilities that would later define many Korean dramas of the 1990s.

Today, both the drama and its soundtrack remain enduring cultural touchstones, remembered for their timeless elegance and lasting contribution to Korean television and popular music.
