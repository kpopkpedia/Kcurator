MONSTA X is officially returning to North America this fall with its highly anticipated **2026 WORLD TOUR [THE X : NEXUS]**. Marking the group's first North American tour in four years since the successful 2022 *NO LIMIT* Tour, the upcoming run will reunite MONSTA X with fans across the continent.

Promoted by Live Nation, the North American leg kicks off on **October 3, 2026, in Fairfax, Virginia**, before traveling through 10 cities across the United States and Canada. The tour will make stops in New York, Boston, Toronto, Rosemont (Chicago area), Irving (Dallas area), Phoenix, Los Angeles, San Francisco, and Seattle.

Five members—**SHOWNU, MINHYUK, KIHYUN, HYUNGWON, and JOOHONEY**—will participate in the tour, while youngest member I.M is currently fulfilling his mandatory military service.

Fans can expect a career-spanning setlist featuring many of MONSTA X's biggest hits alongside songs from the group's third English-language studio album, *Unfold*.

### 2026 'THE X : NEXUS' North America Tour Highlights

- **First North American Tour in Four Years:** MONSTA X returns to North America for its first tour in the region since the 2022 *NO LIMIT* Tour.
- **10-City Itinerary:** The tour spans major concert markets across the East Coast, Midwest, Southwest, West Coast, and Canada.
- **Five-Member Lineup:** SHOWNU, MINHYUK, KIHYUN, HYUNGWON, and JOOHONEY will perform throughout the tour.
- **Career-Spanning Setlist:** Fans can expect songs from *Unfold* alongside many of the group's signature hits.
