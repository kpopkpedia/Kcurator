# About the PUREFLOW Tour

PUREFLOW is LE SSERAFIM's second global concert tour, following **FLAME RISES**, the group's first solo concert tour in 2023.

The tour begins in **Incheon, South Korea**, in July 2026 before continuing across Asia and North America, with additional international dates scheduled afterward. Featuring larger arenas and an expanded global itinerary, PUREFLOW represents LE SSERAFIM's biggest concert production to date.

Fans can expect a performance that blends powerful choreography, cinematic storytelling, live vocals, and dynamic stage production, continuing the group's reputation for delivering high-energy live shows.

# Fan Tips

Before heading to the venue, keep these tips in mind:

## Arrive Early for Merchandise

Official tour merchandise is typically available both outside and inside the venue. Popular items often sell out before the concert begins, so arriving early is recommended.

## Prepare Your Mobile Ticket

Most North American venues require digital tickets through Ticketmaster or another official ticketing platform. Make sure your ticket is downloaded before arriving.

## Review the Venue's Bag Policy

Many arenas enforce clear bag or size restrictions. Check your venue's website before the event to avoid delays.

## Bring Your Official Light Stick

The **FEARNOT BONG** is generally permitted at tour venues, although individual arena policies may vary. Bringing spare batteries is always a good idea.

## Plan Transportation Ahead

Parking near downtown arenas often fills quickly. Public transportation or pre-booked parking can make arrival and departure much easier.

# About LE SSERAFIM

LE SSERAFIM debuted in **May 2022** under **SOURCE MUSIC**, a label of HYBE.

The group consists of five members: **KIM CHAEWON, SAKURA, HUH YUNJIN, KAZUHA, and HONG EUNCHAE**.

Since their debut, LE SSERAFIM has established itself as one of HYBE's flagship girl groups. Known for performance-driven concepts, athletic choreography, and messages centered on confidence, resilience, and self-belief, the group has achieved global success through chart-topping releases, international festivals, and sold-out concert tours.
