About K-pedia

More than K-pop. The Story of Korean Popular Music.

K-pedia is an independent English-language archive documenting the history, artists, industry, soundtracks, concerts, and culture of Korean popular music. From legendary singers and forgotten records to today's global K-pop scene, K-pedia explores the people and stories that shaped Korean music across generations.
