Privacy Policy

At K-pedia, accessible from https://www.kcurator.com/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected and recorded by K-pedia and how that information may be used.

Log Files

K-pedia follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files may include Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring and exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

This information is not linked to information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is to analyze trends, administer the site, track users' movement on the website, and gather demographic information.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many websites, K-pedia uses cookies. These cookies may be used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

The information may be used to optimize visitors' experience by customizing website content based on browser type and other information.

Google Advertising Cookies

Google may act as a third-party vendor on K-pedia. Google and its advertising services may use cookies to serve advertisements to visitors based on their visits to this website and other websites.

Visitors can learn more about how Google uses information and advertising cookies by visiting Google's advertising policies .

Third-Party Privacy Policies

K-pedia's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Visitors are advised to consult the respective Privacy Policies of third-party advertising services or websites for more detailed information about their practices and instructions regarding how to opt out of certain options.

Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Updates to This Policy

K-pedia may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the website, its services, or applicable privacy practices. Any updates will be published on this page.
